SMMT And CAP HPI Publications

New car registrations (monthly), used car sales and the new car and van outlook from SMMT, and the CAP HPI Car Market Overview and Future Car Market Overview reports. Every file found is kept unchanged in S3 (external_data/smmt/, external_data/cap_hpi/) and read into the tables below. Check For New Data reads each source's first page and checks whether recent files were replaced at the same link; Full Scan reads every page of the listings. A check that stores new files sends the CAP HPI and SMMT alerts on Settings > Email Alerts. Where several SMMT files give the same month, the most recent file wins, then your own tracker, then a month worked out from the year to date. Upload Files takes files from your computer (they go in the feed picked here).
